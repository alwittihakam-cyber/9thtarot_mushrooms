A panel can repeat a session on a later flush. Average each panellist's sessions first, so the panellist stays the unit.

## Lab assays: compound content per independent replicate (all protocols)

Unit = one log or plot (caps of the same flush pooled, freeze-dried). Power to detect a ratio between two arms:

| Replicates per arm | 1.3×, CV 15% | 1.5×, CV 15% | 1.3×, CV 30% | 1.5×, CV 30% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 0.38 | 0.71 | 0.13 | 0.25 |
| 4 | 0.57 | 0.89 | 0.20 | 0.38 |
| 6 | 0.79 | 0.99 | 0.28 | 0.57 |
| 8 | 0.91 | 1.00 | 0.40 | 0.72 |
| 10 | 0.96 | 1.00 | 0.47 | 0.83 |
| 12 | 0.99 | 1.00 | 0.56 | 0.89 |

With 3 or 4 replicates only large differences are detectable; 6–8 per arm is the practical minimum for a publishable comparison of compound content.
